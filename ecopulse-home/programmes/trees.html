EcoPulse Foundation's Flagship Program

TREES Program

Training for a Resilient Environment and Empowered Solutions

The TREES program is an environmental education and leadership initiative developed by EcoPulse Foundation to empower young Haitians and prepare them to take action toward a sustainable future.

Through an immersive three-day training experience, participants explore waste management, sustainable agriculture, climate resilience, water conservation, and community leadership.

By combining theory, hands-on workshops, and local engagement, TREES develops a new generation of environmentally responsible leaders ready to create meaningful change for Haiti's environment and society.
