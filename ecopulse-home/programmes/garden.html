School Garden Project

Growing Today, Transforming Tomorrow.

The School Garden Project aims to transform schools into spaces for hands-on, sustainable learning through the establishment of school vegetable gardens.

By growing fruits and vegetables, students will develop agricultural skills, explore environmental challenges, and adopt healthier eating habits.

This initiative seeks to contribute to food security, biodiversity conservation, and stronger connections within school communities across Haiti.

Why This Project?

Cultivating Sustainable Solutions.

In Haiti, environmental degradation, soil erosion, food insecurity, and limited environmental awareness present significant challenges.

The School Garden Project seeks to address these challenges at the school level by combining environmental education, sustainable agricultural practices, and collective participation.

Our vision is to make school gardens accessible educational tools that strengthen young people's knowledge, independence, and sense of responsibility.
