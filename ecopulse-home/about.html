Our Purpose

A Mission Rooted in Community.

Our mission is to promote sustainable practices and empower communities through education, innovation, and positive environmental action.

We believe the most sustainable solutions emerge when young people have the knowledge, tools, and confidence to address challenges within their own communities.

Through our training programs, awareness campaigns, and community cleanup initiatives, we connect learning with action and action with impact.

01

To educate, mobilize, and empower young people and communities to adopt environmentally responsible practices that improve their surroundings and quality of life over the long term.

A Haiti where every community has the knowledge, resources, and opportunities needed to build a healthy, resilient, and self-reliant future.

03

Combining education, local leadership, innovation, and community engagement to develop solutions tailored to local needs and designed to last.

Our Story

From a Vision Shared in Miami to Action Rooted in Haiti.

EcoPulse Foundation was born from a vision of a cleaner, more responsible Haiti, with communities actively engaged in protecting their environment.

The idea was first presented at the Frost Science Museum in Miami by founder Quesnay Lundy, marking the beginning of a dream that would become a reality.

Officially launched in May 2025 in Jérémie, in Haiti's Grand'Anse department, the organization has already implemented several impactful initiatives, including the TREES program, which focuses on educating and mobilizing young people to protect the environment.

This journey reflects a deeply held belief: lasting change begins with young people and education.
