Our Programs.

Environmental Education and Awareness Workshops

Our education and awareness workshops encourage the adoption of environmentally responsible practices and support the transition toward a circular economy focused on preserving and making efficient use of resources.

Community Cleanups

We aim to improve sanitation conditions in vulnerable communities through cleanup activities and waste management. We raise awareness about the importance of hygiene and work toward developing essential sanitation infrastructure.

School Garden Project

The School Garden Project aims to establish school vegetable gardens to promote environmental awareness, encourage healthy eating habits, and develop practical agricultural skills among students in Haiti.

Contact Us

EcoPulse Foundation provides educational programs and environmental initiatives designed to improve community well-being. Our activities include recycling workshops, community cleanup campaigns, and technology training, particularly in electronics and hygiene.

Our goal is to raise awareness, educate, and empower individuals to adopt sustainable practices while creating development opportunities for young people and vulnerable communities.
